# ETHICS

## Ethical and legal scope
FabroGym is an academic ISR-401 project in Ecuador. Public release follows data minimization, pseudonymization and separation of public [P] and restricted [R] evidence in line with the project's ethical package and the Ecuadorian Personal Data Protection framework.

## Consent and restricted evidence
Original consents, signatures, identity documents and identifiable recordings are not part of the open replication package. They belong only in the restricted encrypted evidence workflow required by the course.

A13 and the second-round addendum must **not** be described as signed until the team has obtained the actual signatures. No date is retroactively modified.

## Walkthrough chronology
The six WALK sessions occurred before the OSF registration published on 2026-08-29. They are therefore reported transparently as pre-registration/formative evidence. This limitation is documented in the OSF deviations artifact.

## Member checking
Member checking did occur and is documented using `MC-P01`, `MC-P02`, and `MC-P03`. There is **no audiovisual recording** of that activity. This package does not create or imply that such a recording exists.

## AI/recommender scope
The recommendation/AI component is proposed. The public artifacts must not state that machine-learning recommendations were functionally implemented or empirically evaluated when that did not occur.

## Open-data boundary
The open package may contain anonymized transcripts, anonymized questionnaire responses, coding tables, terminal requirements, traceability, processed results, analysis scripts and manuscript-supporting tables/figures. Identifiable evidence remains outside Zenodo.
